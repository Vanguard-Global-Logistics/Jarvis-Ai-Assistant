- **Owner / decider:** William Lavold

- **Sensitivity:** internal business strategy

## Durable decision

The Octagon is the commercial, shared-workspace add-on for Jarvis. It is not a replacement for Jarvis and is not required for private day-to-day Jarvis use.

- Jarvis remains the owner-facing chief orchestrator and intelligence.
- Hermes remains the governed skill registry, router, dependency resolver, and execution engine.
- The Octagon provides shared rooms, channels, threads, projects, approvals, evidence, and human/agent coordination.
- Throne provisions, licenses, bills, updates, supports, and monitors service health across Octagon installations.
- AEGIS is mandatory and independent in every tier. AEGIS is never a premium feature and Jarvis or Throne cannot weaken or overrule it.
- Each family, business, and company keeps an isolated identity, memory, credential, and knowledge vault by default.
- Throne must not read customer content, credentials, tokens, memories, files, or private computer activity.

## Commercial packaging hypothesis

These are testing hypotheses, not advertised prices or contractual commitments.

1. **Jarvis Core** includes Octagon Lite: one owner plus Jarvis in one private workspace.
2. **Octagon Family** adds governed family collaboration for up to six people; starting hypothesis: $29/month.
3. **Octagon Business Starter** adds five users, approvals, audit evidence, and business skills; starting hypothesis: $99/month.
4. Additional business users: starting hypothesis of $12/user/month.
5. **Octagon Business Pro** adds multi-workspace governance, richer automation, and reporting; starting hypothesis: $249/month.
6. The Hive Exchange is deferred until identity, payment verification, dispute handling, and cross-Hive safety are proven.

Pricing must be validated with real customers, costs, support burden, and willingness-to-pay research before launch.

## Five required differentiators

1. **AEGIS admission and execution security:** deny-by-default permissions, real approval gates, rate limits, sandboxed tools, stop controls, rollback, and externally anchored tamper-evident evidence.
2. **Throne commercial control plane:** tenant provisioning, licensing, billing, signed update staging, recovery, fleet health, and strict content blindness.
3. **Jarvis + Hermes intelligence:** one consistent Jarvis identity using governed Hermes skills and replaceable local or approved model providers.
4. **Governed private and shared memory:** provenance, ownership, sensitivity, correction, deletion, expiry, company isolation, and explicit sharing scopes.
5. **Hive Exchange:** AEGIS-to-AEGIS quarantine before cross-Hive work, mutual admission before any real-world execution, verified paid-customer ratings, anti-Sybil controls, dispute evidence, and reputation based on completed work.

## Marketplace and civic rules

- A rating, thumb up/down, or work review requires a verified completed paid transaction.
- Reviews must attach to the transaction and remain subject to fraud, conflict-of-interest, appeal, and dispute policies.
- Political funding, campaign services, election influence, voter targeting, political persuasion, and coordinated political manipulation are prohibited.
- The Octagon must not market itself as a government, police force, or substitute for law. Enforcement means platform access control, quarantine, evidence preservation, suspension, and lawful reporting.
- Capitalist cooperation is allowed: families and businesses may hire, sell, buy, and combine capabilities under transparent prices, voluntary agreements, and enforceable safety boundaries.

## Autonomy and approval baseline

| Capability | Initial autonomy | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Provision an Octagon workspace | A2 | Owner approval before creation or billing |
| Draft a shared project brief | A1 | Human reviews before sharing |
| Route an approval request | A2 | Named approver decides; no self-approval |
| Draft a customer response | A1 | Human sends until a narrow policy is proven |
| Cross-Hive transaction | A0 / disabled | Remains off until AEGIS, identity, payment, dispute, and audit gates pass |

Every new skill defaults to A1. External messages, publishing, purchases, payments, contracts, credentials, destructive changes, and production releases require explicit approval unless a narrow written policy authorizes that exact reversible action.

## First pilots

Pilot with owner-controlled workspaces before outside sales:

1. Vanguard / Throne owner workspace.
2. Sophisticated Sips family-business workflow.
3. BCI Operations project workflow.

Each pilot must prove tenant isolation, AEGIS denial without bypass, approval handling, no cross-company leakage, tool-failure stopping, owner pause/revoke, measurable time saved, and recoverable rollback.

## Honest current state

This memory records commercial intent and product boundaries. It does not mean the Octagon application, Hive Exchange, billing, multi-tenant identity, or production AEGIS enforcement is complete. The proven Jarvis R13.3 voice baseline remains separate and must not be rewritten by Octagon planning.
