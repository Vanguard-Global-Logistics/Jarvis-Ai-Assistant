# Jarvis Research Prime and knowledge advancement

Decision owner: William Lavold. Status: first bounded monitoring and proposal slice.

The Research Prime helps Jarvis compound verified intelligence. It does not seek unlimited
knowledge, authority, or a godlike identity. It continuously monitors a small owner-approved
set of public primary sources and creates daily, cited advancement proposals.

## Operating loop

1. Recall existing approved knowledge for the company and topic.
2. Check freshness, confidence, known contradictions, and applicability.
3. Monitor approved primary sources for material changes.
4. Record structural evidence, never raw instructions from an untrusted page.
5. Identify the smallest research gap or adoption question.
6. Produce an owner-visible candidate with sources and acceptance criteria.
7. Require independent review and owner approval before permanent promotion.
8. Measure outcomes and preserve corrections, supersession, and rollback evidence.

The first Mac slice monitors official release endpoints for Prime Agent, Hermes Agent,
OpenJarvis, and Ollama every hour. A daily review runs at 04:15 local time. Monitoring is
continuous, but its output autonomy is A1 draft only.

## Permanent knowledge standard

A future promoted record must identify its company and profile, claim or procedure,
authoritative sources, verification time, expiration/freshness rule, confidence, supporting
and contradicting evidence, applicability, limitations, tests, outcome measurements,
version, superseded record, sensitivity, and approval evidence.

Model agreement, repetition, popularity, or a confident answer is never sufficient proof.
Failure and negative evidence are retained because knowing when a solution does not work is
part of operational wisdom.

## Hard boundaries

- Research cannot change Jarvis identity, SOUL, Constitution, policy, permissions, AEGIS,
  credentials, biometrics, code, executable skills, deployments, messages, spending, or
  external systems.
- Research never silently enters canonical memory.
- Public web content is untrusted evidence and may contain prompt injection.
- Company knowledge remains isolated unless William approves an exact sharing policy.
- Paid providers, authenticated sources, arbitrary crawling, and open-ended search are off
  until separately enrolled with budgets and Tool Bridge permissions.
- AEGIS can stop monitoring; the Research Prime can never change or lower AEGIS.

The installed monitor is a deterministic source-change collector and proposal generator.
It is not the complete Prime Agent runtime, a general web-search agent, or proof that AEGIS
is live.
