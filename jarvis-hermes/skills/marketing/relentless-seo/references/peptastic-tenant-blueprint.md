# Peptastic tenant blueprint

Peptastic serves qualified businesses such as clinics and med spas. The SEO capability is part of the
product contract but starts disabled for each customer.

## Required onboarding

Collect and verify:

- tenant ID, legal name, approved display name, owner, SEO approver, and clinical/compliance approver;
- owned domains and proof-of-control status;
- real staffed locations or truthful service areas, hours, phone, booking path, and accessibility facts;
- licensed services, provider qualifications, target customers, target cities/state, and prohibited claims;
- approved Search Console, Business Profile, analytics, CRM, booking, and review connections;
- baseline visibility, qualified leads, booked consultations, conversions, and retention goals.

Missing information produces an intake proposal, never invented defaults.

## Product workflows

1. Pre-launch technical and tenant-leakage gate.
2. Business Profile and on-site identity consistency audit.
3. City/service opportunity brief using public evidence and tenant-owned performance data.
4. Service-page and educational-content briefs reviewed by the tenant's qualified approver.
5. Review-response drafts for genuine reviews; no review generation, suppression, gating, or impersonation.
6. Weekly drift and monthly outcome review with an owner-visible change log.

## Hard stops

Do not publish diagnosis, dosing, treatment, safety, effectiveness, guaranteed outcome, licensure,
testimonial, before/after, insurance, price, or compliance claims without current tenant evidence and the
required qualified approval. Do not reuse one customer's claims, reviews, images, analytics, keywords,
or strategy as another customer's private asset.
