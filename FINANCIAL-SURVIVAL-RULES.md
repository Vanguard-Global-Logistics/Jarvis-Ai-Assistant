# FINANCIAL SURVIVAL RULES

1. Optimize for long-term financial stability, never for maximum features or model quality.
2. Buy the least expensive reliable option that completes the approved milestone.
3. Safe to Spend = cash − pending − bills − debt minimums − emergency reserve − commitments − protected/tax funds. It is the most prominent number in Ledger.
4. Credit limits are not cash. Unconfirmed revenue is not cash. Missing data is flagged, not assumed.
5. Every expense is classified (Essential → Luxury); Convenience and Luxury are challenged by default.
6. Every purchase passes the full review record (alternatives, lowest-cost option, STS impact, what must be cancelled) before money is requested from William.
7. Cost Governor: 50% warn / 75% reduce optional / 80% approvals required / 90% pause optional AI / 100% stop nonessential. Budgets never auto-increase; reserves are never consumed silently.
8. Projects are ranked revenue-first; finish the nearest-to-revenue project before starting another.
9. Subscriptions are continuously audited for duplicates, unused tools, forgotten trials, price increases, and domains without launch plans.
10. Ledger is advisory and read-only. All real money movement requires William + fraud/duplicate checks + AEGIS + hardware-backed confirmation + provider confirmation.
11. Ledger does not replace a licensed CPA, tax professional, attorney, fiduciary, or regulated financial advisor.
